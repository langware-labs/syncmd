Meet syncmd

syncmd treats the copies as one logical asset with several native mount points — and keeps them converged on the latest change.

one asset ⇄
CLAUDE.md AGENTS.md .github/copilot-instructions.md

It's a compiler, not a copier: it works out which member actually changed since the group last agreed — a concurrent edit is reported as a conflict, never silently clobbered.
